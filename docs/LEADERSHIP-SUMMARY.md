# Leadership Summary — How Bookings Work After the Updates

> **Date:** 2026-09-01
> **Audience:** Leadership / non-technical team (several of you are also therapists)
> **Project:** The Wellness Hub — Online Therapy Booking Platform

---

## In short

Here is how the platform behaves now, after the updates. This is a quick, plain-English walk
through of the client side and the therapist side.

---

## For clients (booking)

### Booking one session

1. Open a **therapist's page**.
2. Pick a **session type** — 30, 60, or 90 minutes.
3. The **times shown** match that length (a 60-minute session shows 60-minute slots).
4. The **price** updates and shows in the right currency (EGP or USD).
5. Pick a time and pay. The price you see is the price you pay.

### Booking a repeat series

1. Tick **"Do you want to repeat this appointment?"**.
2. Choose **how often** (weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly) and **how many** sessions.
3. See the **list of upcoming sessions** before you pay.
4. If a time you wanted is taken, the system picks the next free time and tells you.
5. Pay the first session — the rest are created automatically.
6. Before each follow-up, you get a **"pay for your session"** email.
7. If you don't pay one by its date, it is cancelled automatically.

### Payment & currency

- Currency depends on the **therapist** (and your location).
- **EGP** bookings are charged in pounds; **USD** bookings in dollars.
- An **Egyptian client on a USD therapist** pays in pounds (converted at a live rate), but
  the booking is recorded in dollars.
- The amount you see when booking stays the same through checkout and payment.

### Emails you get

- A **confirmation** after booking and after paying.
- Repeat bookings are **labelled** and show all upcoming sessions.
- Times are shown in **your** time zone.

---

## For therapists

### Your bookings

- You land on your **appointments list** after you log in.
- Each booking shows the **client**, session **type**, **time** (in the right time zone), and
  **price**.

### Recurring series

- A repeat booking shows a **"Recurring"** marker.
- Click it to see the **whole series** — all upcoming sessions, not just the first.
- You can open each session from there.

### Emails you get

- A **notification** for each new booking (and each session in a repeat).
- It shows the client, session type, and the full series.
- If a repeat session isn't paid, you get a **cancellation notice** saying so.

---

## For the team (admin)

- Appointments and orders show a clear **"Recurring"** status.
- You can view a whole series and **cancel it in one action** (with an option to waive the
  usual cancellation fee and give a full refund).

---

## Why this matters

- **A clear, reliable booking experience** for clients.
- **Fewer missed follow-ups** — clients are reminded, unpaid ones are handled automatically.
- **Correct charges** — no wrong amounts or flipped currency.
- **Less manual work** — repeat bookings are created and managed by the system.
