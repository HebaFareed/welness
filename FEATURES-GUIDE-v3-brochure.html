Currency Per Therapist

Set EGP or USD per therapist — prices show automatically in their currency. Default is EGP.

Where to Configure

  1. Go to Users → All Users
  2. Click Edit on a therapist account
  3. Scroll to Appointment Timezone
  4. Find Your currency dropdown — directly below timezone
  5. Choose EGP or USD, click Update Profile
Note: The currency dropdown only appears for therapist accounts (shop_staff role). A Currency column also appears in the Users list.

2

Session Types & Durations

Offer multiple session options per product — each with its own label, duration in minutes, and dual-currency pricing (EGP & USD). Clients pick from a dropdown on the booking page.

Where to Configure

  1. Go to Products → All Products
  2. Click Edit on an Appointment product
  3. In Product Data → General tab, scroll to Wellness Product
  4. Check ☑ Enable Duration Options
  5. Click + Add Duration Option and fill each row: Label, Duration (min), EGP Price, USD Price
  6. Click Update to save
Smart fallbacks: Leave Duration blank → uses product default. Leave EGP or USD Price blank → uses original price for that currency.

First-Time Client Intake Form

New clients complete a health & contact questionnaire during checkout. Appears only for emails with no previous orders. Returning clients skip straight to payment — no login needed.

How it decides: 🆕 Email with no past orders → full 7-step checkout · 🔁 Email with any previous order → skips to payment (Step 1 → Step 7) · ✏️ Different email → treated as new

Where to View Submitted Forms
