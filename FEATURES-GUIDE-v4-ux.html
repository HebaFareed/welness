Currency Per Therapist

Set EGP or USD per therapist — prices display automatically in their currency. Default is EGP.

Where to Configure

  1. Go to Users → All Users in the sidebar
  2. Click Edit on a therapist account
  3. Scroll to the Appointment Timezone section
  4. Find Your currency dropdown — directly below the timezone field
  5. Choose EGP or USD, then click Update Profile
Note: The currency dropdown only appears for therapist accounts (shop_staff role). A Currency column also appears in the Users list for quick reference.

Session Types & Durations

Offer multiple session options per product — each with its own label, duration, and dual-currency price (EGP & USD). Clients choose from a dropdown on the booking page.

Products → Edit → General Tab → Wellness Product section

Where to Configure

  1. Go to Products → All Products
  2. Click Edit on an Appointment product
  3. In Product Data → General, scroll to Wellness Product
  4. Check ☑ Enable Duration Options
  5. Click + Add Duration Option — fill Label, Duration (min), EGP Price, USD Price
  6. Click Update to save
Smart fallbacks: Leave Duration blank → uses product default. Leave EGP or USD Price blank → uses the product's original price for that currency. Only fill what differs from defaults.

First-Time Client Intake Form

A health & contact questionnaire that only appears for emails with no previous orders. Returning clients skip to payment automatically — no login required.

How it decides: 🆕 Email with no past orders → full 7-step checkout · 🔁 Email with any order → skips to payment (Step 1 → Step 7) · ✏️ Different email → treated as new

Where to View Submitted Forms

  1. Go to Appointments → Intake Forms in the sidebar
  2. Browse forms by email — click any entry to see all 27 fields in detail
  3. Intake summary also appears on each order page & in New Appointment emails
