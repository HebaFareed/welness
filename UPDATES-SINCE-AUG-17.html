What's new on The Wellness Hub

A short, plain-English round-up of the recent improvements to booking, payments and client information — and what they mean for you and your clients.

Repeat bookings now work properly

Booking a series of sessions is simpler and clearer for everyone — from the first booking to the last payment.

  • ClientChoose to repeat a session — weekly, every two weeks, or monthly — and how many sessions. The full list of upcoming dates is shown before paying.
  • TherapistYour first confirmation email lists the whole series, so you can see the full schedule at a glance.
  • ClientA friendly reminder is emailed the day before each session, so the next one is paid in good time.
  • TherapistYou and the client get a confirmation for every session once it's paid.
  • Site ownerSessions that aren't paid in time are cancelled automatically so they don't block the diary, and the therapist is told.
  • Site ownerThe appointments and orders lists show a “Recurring” label with the full series, and a whole series can be cancelled in one go — with the option to waive the cancellation fee.
�

Prices & payments

Clearer pricing, a smoother payment step, and no surprises about what was charged.

  • ClientPrices still show in the right currency — Egyptian pounds or US dollars — based on your therapist.
  • ClientWhen a US-dollar booking is paid from Egypt, the card is charged in Egyptian pounds through our Egypt card provider. The booking itself stays in US dollars for you and your therapist, and the converted amount is shown on the order.
  • ClientClients paying from outside Egypt are charged in US dollars, as before.
  • ClientA US-dollar booking now always offers a way to pay — previously it could get stuck on the payment step.
In short: you always see the price in your currency, and if a payment has to be converted, the exact amount charged is shown on the order.
⏱️

Session times now match the session length

The times offered when booking now line up with the length of the session chosen.

  • ClientA 60-minute session shows 60-minute times — 30, 60 and 90-minute sessions all line up correctly.
  • ClientPreviously, some longer sessions could show no available times at all. That's fixed.
📝

The client intake form moved to the thank-you page

The health questionnaire no longer stands between the client and paying — it's collected straight after booking, where it can't slow checkout down.

  • ClientBooking is now just two steps: contact details, then payment.
  • ClientFirst-time clients fill in the form on the thank-you page, with their name and mobile already filled in. It takes a couple of minutes.
  • ClientReturning clients are never asked again.
  • ClientIf the form is still missing, a gentle reminder email is sent about a day later.
  • TherapistYour new-appointment email shows the client's answers — or a clear “Intake form: pending” note if it hasn't been completed yet.
�

Online sessions are clearly labelled

Every booking now says how the session takes place, so there's no confusion.

  • Client“Appointment type: Online” appears in the basket, at checkout, on the thank-you page, and in every appointment email.
  • TherapistIt also appears in the appointment details of your emails, so you can see at a glance that the session is online.
🛠️

Fixes & improvements

  • Corrected a currency mix-up that could show the wrong amount on appointment orders.
  • The recurring-series label now shows the right status and opens the full schedule.
  • Fixed an error that could affect how session-type prices were displayed.
  • A US-dollar booking no longer gets stuck on the payment step.
  • The US-dollar to Egyptian-pound conversion is now shown on the order.
  • Polished the wording and layout of several emails so they're easier to read.
